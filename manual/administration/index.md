# Installation and administration

This part is for the person who runs Anthology: installing it, configuring it, adding the first users and the music, and keeping it running.

1. [Installation](./installation) – the pod with Podman Quadlet or Docker/Podman Compose.
2. [Configuration](./configuration) – environment variables, HTTPS and the data volumes.
3. [First steps](./first-steps) – the first admin and the first music.
4. [Users and devices](./users) – accounts, roles and signed-in devices.
5. [Importing a collection](./import) – bringing an existing music folder into the library.
6. [Source services](./sources) – optional services that offer music from other sources.
7. [Raumfeld speakers](./speakers) – making the speakers and the server find each other.
8. [Maintenance](./maintenance) – the system maintenance, quality checks and identification.
9. [Backups and updates](./backup-and-updates) – what to back up and how to update.
