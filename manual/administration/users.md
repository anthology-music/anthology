# Users and devices

## Accounts

Admins manage accounts in **Settings › Accounts**:

- **Create** an account with display name, user name, password and role.
- **Change the role** of an account (listener, editor, admin).
- **Deactivate** an account: it is signed out on every device at once, and its data stays.

There is always at least one active admin.

## Devices

Every browser or app you sign in with is a **device** of your account. **Settings › My devices** lists them with the time they were last used. Sign out a device you no longer use – for example a lost phone – and its access ends immediately.

Settings that depend on the device, such as the interface language and the bitrate for listening, are kept per device.
