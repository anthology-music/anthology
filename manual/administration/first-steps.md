# First steps

## Create the first admin

A fresh installation opens the setup. Enter your name, a user name and a password: this account becomes the first admin.

<Screenshot name="sign-in" alt="The sign-in page" />

Afterwards everyone signs in on the sign-in page. Each browser you sign in with becomes a device of your account (see [Users and devices](./users)).

## Bring in music

You have two ways to fill the library:

- **Import a folder** you already have: Streaming › Import folder. See [Importing a collection](./import).
- **Order albums** from a source service, if you run one: see [Source services](./sources).

After an import, Anthology identifies the recordings with MusicBrainz in the background and fetches covers. Uncertain results appear in the review list.

## Invite your household

Create accounts for everyone in **Settings › Accounts** and choose their role (see [Roles and rights](/general/roles)). Each person signs in with their own account; the library is shared, while playlists, favorites and settings stay personal.
