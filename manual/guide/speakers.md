# Playing on speakers

With Raumfeld speakers in your home, the app becomes a remote control: the music plays on the speakers, even when you close the app.

## Choosing where to play

The **Play on** button in the player lists **This device** and every Raumfeld zone. Choose a zone, and the queue moves there and continues at the same position.

<Screenshot name="output-picker" alt="The list of zones to play on" />

While a zone plays, the player shows **Playing on …** and its buttons and volume control the zone. Switch back to **This device** to listen in the browser again.

## Zones and rooms

**Zones and rooms** shows every zone with its rooms and their volumes. Move a room into another zone, give it its own zone, or take it out of every zone (**Rooms without a zone**). Each room can be muted and has its own volume.

<Screenshot name="zone-manager" alt="Zones and rooms with their volumes" />

If no speakers appear, ask your admin – see [Raumfeld speakers](/administration/speakers).
