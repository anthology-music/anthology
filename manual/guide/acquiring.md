# Acquiring music

**Streaming** is the place to bring new music into the library. Everything you start here runs on the server: you can close the app, and the progress is shown live under **Jobs**.

## Importing a folder

Admins import folders from the server with **Import folder**. Choose the folder and the storage mode, check the report and confirm. See [Importing a collection](/administration/import).

## Ordering from a source

If your installation runs source services, editors and admins can search them or paste a link into **Search or link**.

<Screenshot name="source-search" alt="An album found at a source service, with its tracks" />

1. **Search** for an artist, album or track, or **Open link**. Choose a source, or search all.
2. **Show tracks** of a result. Each track says whether it is **New**, **Already present** or a **Better offer** than the copy you have.
3. Select the tracks, choose the **Quality**, and **Send order**.

The order downloads the tracks and imports them by itself. **Orders** shows each track's state – queued, downloading, waiting for a rate limit, done – and its outcome: new, replaced, already present, not better than the existing copy, or needs review.

**Source status** shows which sources are available and why one is not.
