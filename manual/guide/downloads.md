# Downloads

Download a track with **Download** in its **⋮** menu, or a whole album as a zip file with **Download album**.

Downloads carry the tags of the album you downloaded from, including its cover – even when the same recording appears on other albums with other titles. The files are originals; your bitrate setting does not apply.

If the tagger service is not reachable, Anthology says so and delivers the file without album tags.
