# Browsing the library

**Local** shows the shared library in three sections: **Albums**, **Artists** and **Tracks**. Every list loads progressively; scroll as far as you like, or jump with the scrollbar.

<Screenshot name="library-albums" alt="The albums of the library" />

## Artists and albums

Choose an artist to see their albums, and an album to see its tracks with discs, durations and the quality of each file.

<Screenshot name="library-artists" alt="The artists of the library" />

<Screenshot name="release" alt="An album with its cover and tracks" />

The quality badge shows the **effective quality** of a file: **Hi-res**, **CD** or **Lossy**. A file that claims a better quality than it has is marked as upscaled; **Check** means the analysis was not conclusive.

One recording can appear on several albums – for example on the original album and on a compilation. It is stored only once, and each album shows it with the title and number it has there.

## Search

The search field at the top searches artists, albums and tracks while you type. It ignores case and accents and tolerates small typos.

<Screenshot name="search" alt="Search results for a track title" />

Narrow the search with **filters**, typed or chosen with **Filter**:

| Filter | Example | Finds |
|---|---|---|
| `artist:` | `artist:Mozart` | by artist |
| `album:` | `album:Nordlicht` | by album |
| `composer:` | `composer:Bach` | by composer |
| `genre:` | `genre:Blasmusik` | by genre |
| `year:` | `year:1985`, `year:>2000`, `year:<1970` | by year |
| `format:` | `format:flac` | tracks in a format |
| `quality:` | `quality:lossless` | tracks of a quality |

On an album or artist page, **Search in this album** or **Search this artist** limits the search to it. Results follow changes of the library without searching again.
