# User guide

This part is for everyone who listens to and looks after the music. The app has three areas in its main navigation:

- **Local** – the shared library: albums, artists, tracks, search, and your playlists, favorites and history.
- **Streaming** – acquiring music: importing folders and ordering from source services, with the jobs and their progress.
- **Settings** – this device and your account.

Editors and admins also see the **Review list** and **Conflicts**.
