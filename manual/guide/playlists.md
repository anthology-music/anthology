# Playlists, favorites and history

**Local › My music** holds your personal music: **Playlists**, **Favorites** and **History**. They belong to your account and follow you to every device.

## Playlists

<Screenshot name="playlists" alt="The list of playlists" />

- **Create** a playlist by name, or right from an album with **Add to a playlist** (choose a playlist or **Create and add**). Single tracks have **Add to a playlist …** in their **⋮** menu.
- **Order** the tracks by dragging them, or with the arrow buttons; **remove** tracks you no longer want. Each change can be undone with **Undo**.
- Keep playlists in **folders**.
- A playlist is **private** until you choose **Share with everyone**: then everyone sees it under **Shared by others** and can add, remove and reorder tracks, and each track shows who added it; renaming, sharing and deleting stay with you.
- **Import a playlist file** (M3U, M3U8, PLS, XSPF): Anthology finds the tracks in the library and lists the entries it cannot find. **Export as M3U8** saves a playlist as a file.

## Favorites

The heart marks a track, an album or an artist as a favorite. **Favorites** lists them by kind.

## History

**History** lists what you played, newest first. A track counts once half of it or four minutes have played; the play counts are shown with the tracks.
