# Settings

**Settings** gathers the settings of this device and of your account. Admins and editors find further sections there.

<Screenshot name="settings" alt="The settings page" />

## For everyone

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| Language | the language of the interface on this device (English or German) |
| Playback on this device | the bitrate for listening |
| My devices | your signed-in devices; sign out the ones you no longer use |
| Speakers | the Raumfeld systems and whether they are reachable |
| About Anthology | versions, license and the licenses of all components |

## For editors and admins

Editors see the **Identification (MusicBrainz)**, the **Quality analysis** and the **Change history**. Admins also manage the **Accounts**, the **Sources**, the speaker addresses, the **Referenced originals**, the **Replaced originals**, the **System maintenance** and the **Services** – see [Installation and administration](/administration/).

## About Anthology

**About Anthology** shows the version of the server and of the app, the license (MIT), a link to the source code, and the licenses of every component Anthology uses.

<Screenshot name="about" alt="About Anthology with the versions and licenses" />

If the app's version differs from the server's, reload the page.
