# Signing in

Open the address of your Anthology server, for example `http://music.local:8400`, and sign in with the user name and password you got from your admin.

<Screenshot name="sign-in" alt="The sign-in page" />

You stay signed in on this device. To sign out, open the **account menu** at the top right and choose **Sign out**. Devices you no longer use can be signed out in **Settings › My devices**.

The indicator at the top shows the connection: **Connected · live** means changes from other devices arrive immediately. If the connection drops, the app keeps showing what it has and reconnects by itself.
