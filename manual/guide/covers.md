# Covers and images

Anthology fetches the front cover of every album: from the files themselves, from the Cover Art Archive or from the source an album was ordered from. Covers appear in every list and on the album page.

## Changing the cover

Editors can **Upload cover** on the album page (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB). An uploaded cover is protected: automatic fetching never replaces it. **Remove cover** takes it away, and Anthology falls back to the best automatic one.

## Further images

The cover opens the gallery with the further images of the album: back, booklet, disc and others. Editors can add images, change their kind, order them and remove them.
