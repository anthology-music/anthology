Listening ​

Playing ​

Choose Play on an album to play it from the start, or the play button of a track to play from there. The ⋮ menu of a track offers Play next and Add to queue.

The player at the bottom shows the current track, its position and the volume, with buttons for previous, play/pause and next.

The queue ​

Queue opens the list of the coming tracks. Remove tracks from it, or add more from anywhere in the library. The queue is kept on the server, so it is still there when you reload the page or come back later.

Sound quality ​

Anthology sends each device a format it can play. In Settings › Playback on this device you choose the bitrate: Original plays the files as they are; a lower bitrate saves data on the go. A file is never converted to a higher bitrate than it has.

A track marked Not playable on this device has a format that neither the device nor the server can convert.
